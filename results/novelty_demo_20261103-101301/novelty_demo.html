C4. Checking pays most at moderate degradation

Claim. The value of paying for checks peaks when the tool degrades moderately; it is negative for a nearly healthy tool and falls when switching becomes the better response.

Gap: G3 (how check value changes with degradation severity is not mapped for tool agents).

Live spot check: re-ran 6 saved runs now -> 6/6 identical [OK: the saved results come from this code]

SUPPORTED (pre-registered H-FA1 confirmed): peak +23.3% at 80% success vs -8.3% at 95% and +8.8% at 60% (5% false alarms)

Limit: Simulation; the effect is strong when wrong answers are costly (3 tasks) and small when they cost 1 task.
